Research II — Labor and Undocumented Work

Independent Researcher & Research Assistant Gr. 10–12 · 10 hrs/wk · 36 wks/yr

Graduate School of International Studies (GSIS), Ewha Womans University

  • Learned applied quantitative migration analysis — gravity models, institutional economics, labor migration theory — and statistical analysis in STATA, including data cleaning, regression, and interpretation.
  • Assisted research on Timorese labor migration to South Korea by conducting and analyzing field survey data from migrant workers, empirically evaluating South Korea's Employment Permit System (EPS).

"Where and Why Do the Timorese Migrate? A Gravity Approach and Reference to South Korea"

Gr. 10–11

  • Published in Nakhara: Journal of Environmental Design and Planning (NJEDP); acknowledged as a main contributor.
  • Empirically examined field survey data of migrant workers from East Timor in South Korea.
  • Found that Korea's EPS — not physical distance — plays the key role in determining migrant flows, confirming Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson's Institution Hypothesis.
  • Invited to present at the 8th Asian Conference on Business and Economic Studies, Hanoi, Vietnam.

Research III — Politics and Theory

How Social Media Fuels Populism and Strategies for Counteraction Gr. 10–12 · 10 hrs/wk · 36 wks/yr

First Author (with Daeho Lee & Chehwan Kim) · published in the International Journal of High School Research (peer-reviewed)

  • Part of the Think-Tank Ultimatum (TTU) school curriculum; won the Grand Prize out of 100+ school-wide participants.
